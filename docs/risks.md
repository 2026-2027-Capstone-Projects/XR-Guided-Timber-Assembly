# Risks

**Objective:** Deliver a functional XR-guided timber assembly training application in Unity 6.3 LTS that enables instructors and students to complete training scenarios successfully within the project timeline.

## Active Risks

### R-1 Team, H/M (midterms occur every term; coursework takes priority)
Several team members will have midterm exams during development; if project work is delayed during those periods, planned milestones may not be completed on schedule.

- Owner: SCRUM Master
- Response: Plan sprint workloads around known exam schedules and complete critical tasks before midterm weeks.
- Trigger: Sprint velocity drops below planned capacity during midterm weeks.
- Asked: N/A

### R-2 Team, M/M (illness is unpredictable; work can be redistributed)
Project members may become sick during development; if key contributors are unavailable, feature development and testing may be delayed.

- Owner: Project Manager
- Response: Maintain shared documentation and ensure knowledge is distributed across the team.
- Trigger: A team member is unavailable for more than three days during an active sprint.
- Asked: N/A

### R-3 Technical, M/H (deadlines may compress development time)
Project deadlines may require features to be implemented quickly; if work is rushed, technical debt may accumulate and reduce maintainability or increase defects.

- Owner: Quality Owner
- Response: Enforce code reviews, track technical debt items, and reserve sprint capacity for refactoring.
- Trigger: Features are merged without review or technical debt tasks are repeatedly postponed.
- Asked: N/A

### R-4 Technical, M/M (research is required before implementation)
The project depends on researching XR interaction techniques and OpenXR functionality; if research takes longer than expected, development of dependent features may be delayed.

- Owner: AI Coordinator
- Response: Time-box research activities, document findings, and create proof-of-concept prototypes before full implementation.
- Trigger: Research tasks exceed their planned sprint duration.
- Asked: N/A

### R-5 Technical, M/H (OpenXR integration is partially unproven)
Some OpenXR features required for the training application have not yet been integrated into the project; if integration challenges arise, key training functionality may not be available.

- Owner: AI Coordinator
- Response: Develop prototype implementations early and validate hardware compatibility as soon as possible.
- Trigger: OpenXR proof-of-concept fails to meet requirements by sprint review.
- Asked: N/A

### R-6 Business, M/H (requirements may evolve during development)
Instructor expectations for the training module may change during development; if requirements change significantly, completed work may require substantial rework.

- Owner: Project Manager
- Response: Review requirements regularly with stakeholders and gather feedback on early prototypes.
- Trigger: New requirements invalidate previously completed functionality.
- Asked: Requested feedback during milestone reviews.
